About This Petition

An Independent Audit of Iowa HHS

This petition calls for an independent, external audit of Iowa's Department of Health and Human Services — specifically its child-welfare oversight, corrective-action follow-through, and records-integrity standards.

Iowa's child welfare system has undergone four federal Child and Family Services Review (CFSR) cycles. Each cycle has produced findings, Program Improvement Plans, and commitments to reform. Yet patterns of non-compliance, documentation gaps, and inadequate corrective follow-through have persisted across decades.

This petition is not a legal filing. It is a public transparency effort — grounded in open records, federal documentation, and original analysis — intended to give Iowa families, lawmakers, and oversight bodies a clear, evidence-based picture of where the system continues to fall short.

All source documents referenced in this portal are publicly available or were obtained through Iowa Open Records requests. Nothing here is confidential case information.

About Ashley Marie Meredith

Iowa State Capitol steps, March 2026

Ashley Marie Meredith, MSN
Founder, Iowa Family Rights  |  Pro Se Petitioner  |  Child Welfare Analyst

Ashley Marie Meredith is a registered nurse with a Master of Science in Nursing and the founder of Iowa Family Rights. Her analytical work on Iowa's child welfare system began from direct personal experience with the system — experience that motivated a multi-year, document-grounded investigation into systemic patterns of failure.

She filed six open records requests, reviewed hundreds of pages of federal CFSR documentation across four review cycles, and developed the Post-Permanency Risk Convergence Profile (PPRCP) — an original analytical framework addressing a structural blind spot in both state and federal oversight systems.

The photograph was taken on the steps of the Iowa State Capitol in March 2026, the same evening she photographed the Capitol dome used at the top of this page.
