The requested review extends beyond written policy. Iowa has completed three federal Child and Family Services Review cycles and is now participating in a fourth. Source Integrity and Traceability

Every source is assigned a persistent control identifier and documented by title, issuing body, provenance, status, petition use, andâwhen availableâan official public URL. Original source records are distinguished from excerpts, summaries, analytical products, and packet derivatives so readers can trace significant claims back to their underlying evidence.

Public Access and Privacy

This website presents the public-safe layer of the record. Public documents and open-records productions are linked whenever lawful and practicable. Sealed, confidential, restricted, or privately preserved records are not published merely because they informed an audit question.

Case-linked material is minimized, redacted, or identified by provenance and access limitation rather than exposed publicly. When a source cannot lawfully or responsibly be posted, the record explains its origin and availability without disclosing protected information.

Purpose and Status

The petition is not a lawsuit, judicial filing, or request to relitigate an individual child-welfare case. It is a citizen-initiated audit request and public transparency record intended to help families, lawmakers, auditors, journalists, and oversight bodies examine the evidence, understand the methodology, and independently test the questions presented.

Materials are labeled according to their actual status. Drafts remain drafts until reviewed and approved, source limitations are disclosed, corrections are preserved, and no analytical conclusion substitutes for the independent work of an authorized auditor.
