Current Status

This page supports public awareness of the Iowa HHS audit-request work while preserving appropriate control over source files and internal review drafts.

Audit-request focus

Independent review of oversight, corrective-action, procurement, technology-modernization, and child-welfare accountability issues.

Evidence discipline

Formal materials are maintained through source-controlled review, citation checks, and separate public/private handling rules.

Public materials

Public-facing releases are limited to materials cleared for publication. Internal working drafts are not public releases.

Scope

The work concerns recurring Iowa child-welfare oversight issues, public-records access, corrective-action follow-through, and the relationship between documented federal/state findings and practical accountability.

This page is not a substitute for a formal filing, legal brief, or agency submission. It is a public landing page for orientation and document routing.
