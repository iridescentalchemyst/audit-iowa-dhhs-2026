A citizen-initiated request for independent audit attention to Iowa child-welfare oversight, corrective-action, and records-integrity issues.

Current public posture: this page is a public-facing transparency landing page. The formal audit-review packet, source index, and derivative materials are controlled records and are not posted here.

Current Status

This page supports public awareness of the Iowa DHHS audit-request work while preserving appropriate control over formal packet materials, source files, and review drafts.

Audit-request focus

Independent review of oversight, corrective-action, procurement, technology-modernization, and child-welfare accountability issues.

Evidence discipline

Formal materials are maintained through source-controlled review, citation checks, and separate public/private handling rules.

Public materials

Public releases will be limited to materials cleared for publication. Draft packets and internal working materials are not public releases.

Scope

The work concerns recurring Iowa child-welfare oversight issues, public-records access, corrective-action follow-through, and the relationship between documented federal/state findings and practical accountability.

This page is not a substitute for a formal filing, legal brief, or agency submission. It is a public landing page for orientation and contact.

Materials Handling

Formal packet materials, source indices, review drafts, and derivative reports are not downloadable from this page. They may be routed through appropriate formal submission or review channels when authorized.
