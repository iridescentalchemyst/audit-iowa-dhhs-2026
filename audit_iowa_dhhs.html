Welcome to the Public Transparency Hub

This is the public record for a citizen-initiated Petition to Audit the Iowa Department of Health and Human Services. It documents the petition’s development, materials delivered for preliminary review, and any verified acknowledgments, responses, or follow-up from public officials and oversight bodies.

On August 25, 2026, Ashley Marie Meredith delivered a 62-page Current-State Auditor Review Packet to Iowa Auditor of State Rob Sand during an in-person exchange in Independence, Iowa. The packet presents the stable, review-ready petition core and selected supporting materials as they existed on the date of the handoff.

That handoff was an important step, but it was not the formal petition submission and does not represent acceptance, endorsement, or the opening of an audit. Readers can review the packet below or read Ashley’s account of getting the evidence record into the right hands.

The formal petition remains in final preparation while the remaining sections, attachments, companion reports, and public-source links undergo final verification. This page will be updated as additional materials are finalized and any verified response or follow-up occurs.
