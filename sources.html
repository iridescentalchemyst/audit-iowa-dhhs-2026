Public Source Library

Publicly available source documents and reference materials supporting the Iowa HHS Audit Request.

This page collects publicly available source documents, government reports, public-record portals, oversight materials, statutes, research materials, and contextual references used to support the Iowa HHS Audit Request. This is a source library, not the final Petition to Audit packet. Inclusion of a source does not imply endorsement, acceptance, agreement, or action by the issuing agency, public office, organization, or publisher.

Sources are grouped by issuing body or source type. Public government reports and statutes are treated differently from media or contextual reporting. Media sources are listed only as contextual references and do not independently establish audit findings.

This library is limited to public-facing source materials. It does not publish private correspondence, sealed/confidential records, internal paths, private file links, unreviewed working files, sensitive case materials, or old draft packet PDFs. Additional sources may be added after source review and public-release screening.
