What This Is

The Case for an Independent Audit

Since 2003, Iowa’s child welfare system has been reviewed across multiple federal Child and Family Services Review cycles. The Iowa HHS Audit Request organizes public records, federal review findings, open-records correspondence, and source-backed analysis into a public-facing transparency structure. The formal Petition to Audit has not yet been submitted; this hub currently provides a public-safe current-state packet and a verified index of the audit framework.

Analytical Framework

Domains and Deficiency Categories

The audit framework organizes the source record across analytical domains and recurring deficiency categories. This structure helps distinguish isolated incidents from repeated system-pattern evidence.

Eight Analytical Domains

A

Funding Authorization and Federal Compliance

Federal and state child welfare funding streams, reimbursement compliance, budget allocation, and financial oversight controls.

B

Contract and Provider Oversight

Oversight, monitoring, enforcement, and accountability mechanisms governing contracted providers and service-delivery entities.

C

Decision Controls and Case Authorization

Policies, decision-support tools, controls governing case actions, removals, placements, service authorization, and ICWA compliance.

D

Judicial Interface and Legal Oversight

Agency-court interaction, evidentiary submissions, guardian ad litem coordination, ICWA tribal notification, and oversight mechanisms.

E

Outcomes, Incentives, and Systemic Disparities

Measured child welfare outcomes, performance incentives, demographic disparities, and operational metrics including safety, permanency, and well-being tracking.

F

Workforce Capacity and Internal Accountability

Workforce stability, staffing, supervision systems, training adequacy, internal accountability, and ICWA-specific training requirements.

G

Federal Oversight History and Corrective Action Response

CFSR cycles, Program Improvement Plans, federal oversight findings, corrective-action implementation, and recurring findings across review cycles.

H

Data Integrity and System Auditability

Reliability, accessibility, auditability, and governance of administrative and case-management data systems, including audit trails and reporting infrastructure.

Thirteen Deficiency Categories

# Category Description
1 Repeated CFSR Non-Conformity Findings of non-conformity recurring across multiple federal review cycles without sustained corrective action.
2 PIP Non-Implementation Program Improvement Plans accepted but not fully implemented or independently verified.
3 Safety Assessment Failure Documented patterns of inadequate safety-assessment accuracy in structured decision-making tools and practice.
4 Contract Oversight Gaps Insufficient monitoring, performance accountability, or enforcement in contracted provider relationships.
5 ICWA Compliance Deficiencies Failures in Indian Child Welfare Act notification, tribal coordination, and placement-preference compliance.
6 Post-Permanency Risk Gaps Inadequate monitoring, support, or accountability for children after reunification, adoption, or guardianship.
7 Workforce and Supervision Instability High turnover, supervision gaps, inadequate training, and workforce-capacity deficiencies affecting case quality.
8 Data Integrity and Auditability Concerns Unreliable, inaccessible, or non-auditable administrative and case-management data systems.
9 Fiscal Accountability Gaps Federal funding compliance questions, reimbursement accuracy concerns, and budget-oversight deficiencies.
10 Technology Modernization Risk Data-governance, auditability, and public-records risks introduced by CCWIS/VISION modernization work.
11 Judicial Coordination Deficiencies Gaps in agency-court information sharing, evidentiary standards, and oversight coordination.
12 Child Fatality Pattern Issues Systemic concerns identified in child fatality review records, including prior-contact and oversight patterns.
13 Family Preservation and Stabilization Gaps Insufficient investment in, or implementation of, family-stabilization and preservation services prior to removal.

CFSR Round 1 identifies major Iowa child-welfare performance and systemic-factor deficiencies.
2010–2011
